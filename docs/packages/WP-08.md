# WP-08 — Liquidity mesh, netting, attestations, testnet anchor

Owner: Orchestrator · Branch: `wp-08-mesh` · Wave 2 · Test quota: 70 · Features: **N14, N20 data, Q4 attestations**, on-chain bonus
Owns: `backend/app/engines/mesh/`, `backend/app/chain/`, `backend/app/api/mesh.py`, `backend/tests/mesh/`
Depends on: WP-01, WP-11 (approvals), WP-03 (tokens only on chain).

## Milestones
1. **Obligation graph** across tenants that opted in: who owes whom, amount, due date, status.
2. **Netting engine:** detect cycles; compute a multilateral netting round that minimises cash
   moved (min-cost flow, networkx). Invariants: every party's net position unchanged; no one pays
   more than they owed; total gross ≥ total net.
3. **Consent workflow:** every party approves (EXECUTE tier, step-up); any rejection voids the
   round; expiry after 48 h.
4. **Settlement record** + receipts per party; residual gap routed to the cheapest path
   (WP-06 interface).
5. **Counterparty attestations (Q4):** both sides confirm an invoice/payment → attestation record.
6. **Sepolia anchor:** hash of each settled round (and daily audit-chain head from WP-11) written
   to Ethereum Sepolia via web3.py; store tx hash; verify endpoint recomputes and compares.
   **Hashes only — never tokens, names or amounts on chain.** Offline mode for tests.
7. **Lender events** emitted to WP-06's LenderProfile.
8. **API:** `GET /mesh/obligations`, `POST /mesh/rounds/propose`, `POST /mesh/rounds/{id}/approve`,
   `GET /mesh/rounds/{id}/verify`.

## Tests (≥ 70)
Netting invariants as Hypothesis properties on random graphs; hand cases (3-ring, 5-party);
consent edge cases (reject, expiry, double approve); hash determinism; verify detects any edited
field; nothing personal in the anchored payload; offline chain stub.

## Notes
- Demo ring (synthetic): Tk 11.5 lakh of circular debt cleared with Tk 1.9 lakh of cash along
  debts, or Tk 1.3 lakh with new links.
- Digest = SHA-256 over a per-round random salt + canonical JSON. The salt stays off-chain, so
  nobody can brute-force small amounts from the public hash.
- API visibility: a business sees its own obligations plus those inside its netting group; rounds only by participants (404 otherwise). The salt never leaves the server. No opt-in flow yet: the only stored obligations are the synthetic demo ring, seeded on first use by a synthetic tenant.
- Residual routing picks min total cost, not WP-06's balanced rank: for a short bridge the
  owner pays taka, and the slower-but-cheaper option is flagged `cheaper_than_top_ranked`.
  Cash on hand comes from the fact ledger; a tenant without that fact is treated as having
  none (fail closed).
