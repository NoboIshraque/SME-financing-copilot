# WP-04 — Cash engine: forecast, gaps, alerts, smoothing

Owner: A1 (PC-1) · Branch: `wp-04-cash` · Wave 1–2 · Test quota: 120 · Features: **Q1, Q3**, supports N12, N15
Owns: `backend/app/engines/cash/`, `backend/app/api/cash.py`, `backend/tests/cash/`
Depends on: WP-01. Uses the payment-delay model from WP-17 when present (fallback: empirical per-customer delay distribution).

## Goal
See every cash gap weeks before it happens, explain why, and propose the cheapest fixes.

## Milestones
1. **Baseline forecaster:** daily inflows/outflows for 91 days. Recurring sales with day-of-week,
   month-end and festival calendar (Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Adha, Pohela Boishakh dates for 2026–2027
   in a data file). Known outflows from obligations and bills. Expected collections = open
   invoices × each customer's delay distribution.
2. **Monte Carlo (1,000 paths, seeded):** sample sales noise, collection delays, cost shocks →
   P10/P50/P90 balance per day; per day P(balance < buffer).
3. **Gap detection:** first crossing date, expected shortfall (Tk), lead time, top 3 drivers
   (which customer/bill), plus leading indicators (DSO trend, top customer slowing, stock build,
   sales below seasonal norm).
4. **Alerts:** tiers Watch / Warning / Critical with the exact thresholds in FEATURES.md Q1;
   dedupe unless the forecast moves > 10% or > 3 days; cooldown.
5. **Smoothing levers:** re-time payables within terms, stagger purchases, request advance,
   early-pay discount (only if cheaper than best financing option — call WP-06 interface),
   netting (WP-08 interface), standby facility. Each lever returns its effect on the gap and its
   cost, as an ActionCard (tier DRAFT/EXECUTE).
6. **Buffer target:** amount covering the worst dip in 19 of 20 paths; surplus-sweep suggestion.
7. **Backtest harness** (N15): walk-forward on historical data — gap hit rate, lead time, MAPE —
   runnable on synthetic and on real public data from WP-17.
8. **API:** `GET /cash/forecast`, `GET /cash/gaps`, `GET /cash/alerts`, `POST /cash/whatif`.

## Tests (≥ 120)
Hand-computed small cases; bands never cross (property); deterministic seed; festival effect
present; alert tier boundary table; dedupe/cooldown; every lever reduces the gap it claims to
(property); levers never cost more than claimed; backtest never sees future rows (leakage test);
every output figure has provenance.
