Owner: Labib (human) · Wave 1–2 · Features: **P7**
Owns: nothing in the repo directly; anonymised outputs land in `data/real_anonymised/` via WP-03 tooling.

## Rules
- Consent first, signed. Raw files only in the private team Google Drive, never in the repo, never in chat.
- Run the anonymiser before any derived file enters the repo. Delete raw files after the competition.

## Consent text (read aloud in Bangla, sign the English copy)
"We are students from BUP building a free tool to help small shops manage cash and find fair
finance, for a university competition. With your permission we will use copies of your payment
statements and ledger pages. We will remove your name, phone numbers, your customers' names and
anything that identifies you before using them. Nothing will be published that identifies you.
You can withdraw at any time by calling [Labib's number]."
Name / shop / date / signature.

## Interview (5 minutes)
1. When in the month or year is cash tightest? Why?
2. How much is owed to you right now, roughly, and by how many customers?
3. Have you borrowed for the business? From whom, at what cost, and what was hard?
4. How do you remind customers to pay? What happens when you do?
5. Would you trust an app that drafts reminders and compares loans for you? What would make you trust it?

## Collect
- Merchant statement export (bKash/Nagad), 3–6 months, if they agree.
- Photos of 2–4 baki-khata pages.
- Typical input prices and 2–3 suppliers.
- One quote we may use, anonymised ("— grocery owner, Mirpur").
