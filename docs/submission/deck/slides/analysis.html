Area C, analysis. Hishab builds cash-basis statements and thirteen ratios, each one clickable down to the transactions, and it compares decisions before the owner makes them. Here are five options for the same hardware shop, each replayed on the same thousand paths across forty economic states. A loan barely helps, because it can only pay out after the gap starts. Offering one large customer two percent off for paying early costs 7,600 taka and cuts the risk of dipping below the buffer from 94 to 52 percent. Then we stress-test the advice: it held in 8 of 10 re-runs, and it never changed when we only shuffled rows, renamed customers or reworded the question.
