Security is about one promise: money never moves without the owner. Every action has a tier. Anything that leaves Hishab is EXECUTE: the owner approves that exact card with a fresh one-time code, within 24 hours, and the policy is checked again when it runs. Four actions are denied to everyone, including the owner. The audit log is a hash chain the database refuses to edit. Only anonymised text can reach a model. Our injection guard catches all 96 attacks in our corpus with no false alarms, but we wrote it having seen that corpus, and one new Bangla paraphrase did get through. The copilot still could not act on it, because its tools cannot execute anything.
