What is not proven yet, and what comes next

Honest limits

  • All demo data is synthetic; no real shop has used Hishab yet
  • The injection guard is rules only; new paraphrases are unmeasured
  • The copilot was measured offline, not with live models at scale
  • Not built: receivables and reminders; cut: market watch

Next

  • Pilot with consenting Dhaka shops, from their own bKash and Nagad exports
  • Receivables and reminder drafts in the approval queue
  • Licensed lenders to fund netting and confirm track records
  • An adversarial test suite for the guard and the copilot
We want to be clear about what is not proven yet. All demo data is synthetic, and no real shop has used Hishab. The injection guard is rules only, and we have not measured it on new paraphrases. The copilot was measured offline, not with live models at scale. Receivables and reminder messages are not built, and we cut market watch to finish on time. Next, we want to pilot with consenting Dhaka shops using their own bKash and Nagad exports, add receivables and reminder drafts, bring in licensed lenders to fund netting rounds, and build an adversarial test suite for the guard and the copilot.
