What is not proven yet, and what comes next

Honest limits

  • All demo data is synthetic; no real shop has used DecisionGuard yet
  • The injection guard is rules only: on a fresh held-out set it caught 15 of 24 attacks and flagged 10 of 24 innocent questions
  • The copilot was measured offline, not with live models at scale
  • Reduced: receivables and message drafts (no sending); cut: market watch

Next

  • Pilot with consenting Dhaka shops, from their own bKash and Nagad exports
  • Sending approved reminders through bKash and SMS partners
  • Licensed lenders to fund netting and confirm track records
  • A learned injection classifier beside the rules, and an external security review
We want to be clear about what is not proven yet. All demo data is synthetic, and no real shop has used DecisionGuard. The injection guard is rules only: on a fresh held-out set it caught 15 of 24 attacks and wrongly flagged 10 of 24 innocent questions, so the approval rules, not the guard, are what keep money safe. The copilot was measured offline, not with live models at scale. Receivables and message drafts are built in reduced form, with no sending, and we cut market watch to finish on time. Next, we want to pilot with consenting Dhaka shops using their own bKash and Nagad exports, send approved reminders through bKash and SMS partners, bring in licensed lenders to fund netting rounds, and add a learned injection classifier beside the rules, with an external security review.
