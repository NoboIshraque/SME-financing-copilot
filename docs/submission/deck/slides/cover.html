DecisionGuard

A finance copilot for small Bangladeshi shops. Code computes every number; the model only explains it, and the owner approves every action.

Nobo Ishraque · Amdad · Labib — Bangladesh University of Professionals, Dhaka

Assalamu alaikum, and good evening. We are team DecisionGuard from the Bangladesh University of Professionals. DecisionGuard means “accounts” in Bangla: the word a shop owner uses for the notebook where they track who owes what. DecisionGuard is a finance copilot for small Bangladeshi shops. It covers the three Topic E areas: financing that fits, cash-flow alerts, and business analysis. One rule shapes everything you will see: code computes every number, the language model only explains it, and nothing that matters happens until the owner approves it.
