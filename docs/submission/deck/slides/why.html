Research summary: technical documentation §1

Why not just put a chatbot on top of the ledger? Because language models are not calculators. Research on financial tasks shows they retrieve the wrong figure, rarely catch their own mistakes, and sound more confident than they are. With money, a guessed number becomes a wrong decision. So DecisionGuard splits the work. Code computes every number, with exact decimal arithmetic and a record of where each number came from. The model only plans and explains. And any number the model says is recomputed before the owner sees it.
