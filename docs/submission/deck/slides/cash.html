Area B, cash flow. DecisionGuard forecasts thirteen weeks ahead with a thousand simulated paths, built from the shop’s own weekday, month-end and Eid patterns and from how late each customer usually pays. Here is our synthetic hardware shop: an 86.7 percent chance of a cash gap that starts in five days, with an expected shortfall of about 73 thousand taka. Every possible fix is measured on the same thousand paths, so the difference is the fix, not luck. The cheapest one, asking a buyer to pay early, closes almost the whole gap at no cost, and it arrives as an action card for the owner to approve.
