Every shop in our demo is synthetic and labelled so: five invented Dhaka businesses, 18 months of daily records

Picture the owner of a grocery in Mirpur. Her money moves through bKash and Nagad, a bank account and a cash box, and her credit sales live in a paper baki register. She has three questions, and they are exactly the three areas of Topic E. Which loan is really the cheapest, and can I get it? Will I run short of cash, when, and what can I do about it today? And is my business actually doing well? Everything you will see runs on five synthetic Dhaka shops with eighteen months of daily records, clearly labelled as synthetic.
