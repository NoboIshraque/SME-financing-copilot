Area A, financing. Lenders quote headline rates, so Hishab turns every option into dated cash flows and solves for the true yearly cost, fees included. The same 9 percent headline costs 11.29 percent a year as a normal bank loan, and 22.15 percent as a digital loan repaid in daily debits, because the shop pays interest on money it has already paid back. Our catalogue holds 13 real Bangladeshi products, each with its source link, a quoted line and the date we checked it. If we don’t hold a fact a lender asks for, Hishab says “not on record”: it never assumes a pass. And it shows that “2 percent off for paying 10 days early” is a 109 percent loan.
