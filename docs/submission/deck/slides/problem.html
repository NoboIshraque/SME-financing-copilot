The problem is the same in Dhaka and in Shenzhen. Small firms are short of credit: the IFC puts the gap at 5.7 trillion dollars across developing economies, and about 2.8 billion dollars in Bangladesh. Women-led firms get only 7.28 percent of Bangladesh’s small-business credit. And cash gets stuck: in a Peking University survey of 9,252 Chinese small firms, 71 percent were owed money they had not been paid. Lenders say no because these shops cannot show a clean record, not because the businesses are bad.
