Here is the whole pipeline. The shop’s own records come in: mobile-money and bank statements, the cash book, the baki register, even a photo of a receipt, read on our server. They become typed facts: Bangla and Banglish are read correctly, names and phone numbers become tokens, and every row remembers where it came from. Deterministic engines compute everything from those facts. The copilot plans and explains, and every number it states is recomputed first. Anything that leaves DecisionGuard needs the owner’s approval with a one-time code. And any number on any screen can be tapped to show its formula and the rows behind it.
